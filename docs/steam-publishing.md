# Publishing Sakura on Steam

Checked against Valve's public documentation on 13 October 2026.

1. Register at [Steamworks / Steam Direct](https://partner.steamgames.com/steamdirect). Complete the distribution agreements, legal identity, bank and tax details. Valve charges US$100 per separately published product; two independent Sakura products would normally mean two app fees. [Onboarding](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/onboarding).
2. Create the app as software. Valve accepts certain non-game categories, including Animation & Modeling and Audio/Video Production. Those appear relevant to Sakura VTuber, but Valve decides acceptance. [Accepted software categories](https://partner.steamgames.com/steamdirect).
3. Prepare a Steam-specific application folder, not just the setup EXE. Upload the launcher plus the installed Sakura runtime through SteamPipe, create the Windows depot, and configure the launcher as the launch option. Steam handles installing/updating that depot. Keep user settings, API keys, downloaded voices and imported models out of the depot. [SteamPipe upload and launch configuration](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/sdk/uploading).
4. Prepare the store description, screenshots, trailer, capsule artwork, system requirements, pricing and support contacts. Describe phone pairing and any Gemini/internet costs clearly. Only demonstrate models and artwork you have permission to distribute or use commercially.
5. Complete the content survey accurately. Sakura Companion generates replies at runtime, so disclose its live AI use and the applicable safeguards. Disclose shipped AI-generated artwork/audio when relevant. The tracking-only product should describe what it actually ships, including Chat Reader voices. [Content survey](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/contentsurvey).
6. Submit both the store page and build for review, then publish a Coming Soon page. Valve's current onboarding page specifies a 21-day app-fee waiting period for the first few releases and at least two weeks of public Coming Soon visibility. Check your actual Steamworks release checklist before selecting a date. [Timing requirements](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/onboarding), [review process](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/review_process).
7. Test the Steam-installed build on a clean Windows device before release. Review redistribution rights and notices for PMX Editor, Live2D components, tracking runtimes and other included dependencies. Steam requires adequate rights to all shipped content. Imported user models are excluded from Sakura installers.

Start with Sakura VTuber as a clearly described software product if that matches your intended audience. Keep the Android controller APK available as its companion download; it is not a substitute for the PC depot. No Steamworks account, fee payment, AppID or depot upload has been created by this workflow.
