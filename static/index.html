Automatic bone check

Local model-only snapshots accompany measured bone checks. The snapshot/check button repairs confirmed hair-chain distortion immediately and saves it for later loads. Reset or unfreeze a bone to restore its motion.

Auto-expression mappings

Enable the parameters you want to include, adjust their values, then preview and save. Other parameters remain controlled by tracking and active expressions.

Expressions stay active together until you turn them off. Use Turn off on an individual expression, or press its shortcut again. If two expressions control the same parameter, the latest enabled expression is applied last.
