Model Settings

Model zoom%

Checks colour, shine/sphere and toon textures across the whole model and captures full-body before/after images. Rotate to the back or side before checking; your view stays in place. For PMX, the selected file also opens in PMX Editor. Confirmed texture paths are repaired automatically; missing or ambiguous artwork needs your selection.

Character Bloom:0%
Soft glow around bright highlights. Set to 0 to turn off.
Boosts supported materials' highlights. 0 preserves the model's original finish.

Character lighting

Target: full model. Pick a part to limit gloss and bloom to it.

Automatic bone check

Local model-only snapshots accompany measured bone checks. The snapshot/check button repairs confirmed hair-chain distortion immediately and saves it for later loads. Reset or unfreeze a bone to restore its motion.

Auto-expression mappings

Enable the parameters you want to include, adjust their values, then preview and save. Other parameters remain controlled by tracking and active expressions.

Expressions stay active together until you turn them off. Use Turn off on an individual expression, or press its shortcut again. If two expressions control the same parameter, the latest enabled expression is applied last.

One tick enables camera mouth tracking and local microphone lip sync together. Untick to stop microphone capture and disable both mouth inputs. Face and hand tracking remain available.

Microphone mode uses the input selected in Camera and Audio. It animates the mouth without speaking or loading AI. Frequency mode estimates shapes; it does not recognize phonemes.

Live2D facial expression mappings

VTuber · Webcam tracking

Use your expressions, gaze and head to animate the character. The body-tracking switch also controls shoulders, arms, hands and fingers. Chat AI, speech and screen reactions are paused and the local language model is unloaded.

Face/head tracking and body lean/up-down stay active with normal tracking. This separate option controls only hand-driven arms and fingers.

Twitch authorization

Twitch needs your account username and a user OAuth token with chat:read permission (not your password). Sakura validates the account and permission, then saves the token encrypted on this PC. Leave both fields blank to reuse it. YouTube only needs its livestream link. Twitch sign-in inside Sakura is not implemented.

Chat Reader reads YouTube or Twitch messages aloud using local speech. It turns off face and hand tracking. No AI replies. Internet is required to receive live chat.
