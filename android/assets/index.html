How would you like to use Sakura?

Local · runs on this phone · PC connection not required

Mic off
Local settings and models
Saved area for news; name another place in your question to override. API replies and voices use internet and your provider’s quota. Keys are encrypted in this phone’s private storage. Female and Male voices are saved for your next launch.

Location & nearby places

Location off · ask about nearby restaurants or enable it here.

Your current area can be included in Gemini/OpenAI replies. Android services resolve the area; coordinates go to OpenStreetMap/Overpass for nearby places and Open-Meteo for local weather. No location history is saved, and location stops when the app goes into the background. Choose Precise in Android for better nearby distances.

Wake phrase

Off by default · download the optional wake pack to enable.

Say the phrase, wait for the tone, then ask your question. Background speech currently supports English. Detection and transcription stay on your phone; recognized questions go to your saved AI provider. A persistent notification has Stop listening. Uses extra battery and memory when enabled; the avatar pauses in the background. Turn listening off before changing the phrase. After force-stop, restart or Android stopping the service, open Sakura and enable it again. Background app-opening, calls and location requests require opening Sakura.

At least one model must be saved on this phone. Adding a model connects to the PC and opens its folder/file picker automatically. Once received, the model loads from phone storage wherever you are.

After transfer, the model stays on this phone. The PC can be disconnected. Large desktop models may exceed your phone’s memory.
Dictation uses your phone’s speech service. Review the words before sending. In full screen, the red microphone sends your spoken message automatically, then switches off. Tap again while listening to cancel.
Both devices must use the same trusted home Wi-Fi. The PC app must stay open. Known devices reconnect without another code until forgotten on the PC.

Companion camera

Camera stays off until you start one of these modes. Object recognition shares images with the PC assistant. Face tracking processes video on this phone, sends face measurements to the paired PC, and keeps Companion AI available.

Camera images are sent to the paired PC only in object recognition mode. Face-tracking video stays on the phone; only tracking measurements are sent. Stop camera access at any time with the button.
