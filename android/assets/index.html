How would you like to use Sakura?

Local · runs on this phone · PC connection not required

Local settings and models
API replies and voices use internet and your provider’s quota. Keys are encrypted in this phone’s private storage. Female and Male voices are saved for your next launch.

At least one model must be saved on this phone. Adding a model connects to the PC and opens its folder/file picker automatically. Once received, the model loads from phone storage wherever you are.

After transfer, the model stays on this phone. The PC can be disconnected. Large desktop models may exceed your phone’s memory.
Dictation uses your phone’s speech service. Review the words before sending.
Both devices must use the same trusted home Wi-Fi. The PC app must stay open. Known devices reconnect without another code until forgotten on the PC.

Not connected

Companion

The key is saved on your PC, not in this APK. Google OAuth requires a separate project setup.

Companion camera

Let the PC assistant see you or an object. This does not start VTuber tracking.

Uses the camera selected in the VTuber tab (front/rear). JPEG images go to the paired PC and, when used for a reply, its selected AI provider. Off by default.

Expressions

Phone face tracking

Uses your selected camera for head, eyes and mouth. Face measurements are sent to the PC; camera video is not uploaded. Hand tracking is not included in this first phone build.
